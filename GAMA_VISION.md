# 🌟 SIRIUS LOCAL AI GAMA – Vision 1.0

SIRIUS LOCAL AI GAMA is the official **mobile expansion** of the SIRIUS ecosystem.

Its purpose is to bring:
- offline intelligence  
- offline reasoning  
- offline schoolwork support  
- offline image understanding  
- offline knowledge packs  
- identity‑aware safety  

…to **Android and iOS devices**, without cloud, telemetry, or external dependencies.

## 🎯 Core Principles
- 100% offline  
- deterministic  
- family‑safe  
- privacy‑first  
- modular  
- extensible  
- ARM‑optimized  

## 🚀 Long‑Term Goal
Create the first **fully local mobile AI assistant** that works:
- without internet  
- without cloud APIs  
- without remote servers  
- without data leaving the device  

A personal AI that lives **on your phone**, not in the cloud.
